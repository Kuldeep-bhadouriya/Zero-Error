## Design Context

### Users
Mixed audience of ZE Club participants (students, gamers, and members) using the dashboard to quickly check status, rank movement, mission opportunities, and progression signals.

### Brand Personality
Premium, competitive, and focused. The interface should feel like a polished esports command panel rather than a playful arcade toy.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark, high-contrast, red-accented gaming identity already present in the product. Keep the current outer card look, but make interior content denser, more structured, and more informative with premium esports styling. Avoid AI-like gimmicks, especially big bold emojis.

### Design Principles
1. Preserve the existing shell: keep card exteriors, grid rhythm, and overall red/black visual language stable.
2. Enrich interior information density: combine key stats with compact supporting context, not just headline numbers.
3. Use purposeful hierarchy: clear top labels, meaningful secondary metrics, and concise helper text.
4. Motion should be medium and functional: subtle reveals and feedback that guide attention without distraction.
5. No gimmicks: avoid oversized emoji/icon noise and templated AI-looking patterns.
